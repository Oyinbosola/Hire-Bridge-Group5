# HIRE BRIDGE 🤝💼

Hire Smart. Hire Fast.
Your Talent Matchmaker
Bridging the gap between recruiters and candidates across Nigeria

---

## Our Vision ✊

To become the #1 global platform for recruiters and job seekers — where hiring is transparent, skill-based, and free from ghosting, bias, and uncertainty.

---

## Our Mission 🛠️

To help recruiters find the right candidates quickly and give applicants transparency, guidance, and a fair experience

---

## ✨ Features

- 📱 Fully responsive (desktop, tablet, mobile)
- 🎨 Clean and modern UI design
- 🧭 Structured layout (Hero, Data, Visuals, Table, Form)
- ✍️ Filtering and Dashboards
- 📝 Form with validation and success modal
- 💎 Premium input styling and micro-interactions
- 🧭 Smooth UI interactions (hover, active)
